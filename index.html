A little field guide

⌂ Make yourself at home. Click the ground to walk. Click furniture to eat, sleep, shower, read, and relax. Doors are passable; solid walls and furniture block paths.

▧ Build your own place. Open Build, pick Room, and drag a rectangle of at least 3 × 3 tiles. Rooms include floors, walls, and an entrance. Add doors to connect rooms. Or lay individual floors and walls.

▤ Fill it with your favorites. Open Furnish, pick an item, then click a clear floor. Press R to rotate. Sell refunds 65% of the original furniture price. Cancel actions before changing your home.

▱ Make a living. Choose a job in Work, then start a shift. You receive pay when you finish. Every three completed shifts earns a raise. Bills arrive every three game days.

♡ Take care. Six needs fall over time. Use furniture or quick actions to fill them. Low needs affect mood; your character will never die.

◷ Set your pace. Space pauses. Keys 1 / 2 / 3 change speed. Building pauses time. Scroll to zoom, use Q / E to rotate, and arrow keys or drag with the right mouse button to pan.

↧ Keep your story. Autosaves stay in this browser. Use Download save and Load a save file to move between devices.
